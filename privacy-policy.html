1. Overview

SAVORLEN operates this website to present its brand and published product information. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the website or contact SAVORLEN, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

The public website does not include a contact form, user accounts, or an online checkout. Contact happens through the email, telephone, and WhatsApp links shown on the site.

2. Information you provide

When you contact SAVORLEN, you may provide information such as your name, contact details, the content of your message, and any product or enquiry details you choose to include.

Please share only the information needed for your enquiry. Do not send sensitive personal, financial, or confidential information through email or WhatsApp unless it is necessary and you are comfortable doing so.

3. Information collected automatically

The hosting provider for this website may automatically record technical information in server logs, such as an IP address, browser type, requested pages, referring pages, and timestamps. This information may be used for security, reliability, and understanding how the site is accessed.

SAVORLEN does not currently use this website to build user profiles or to track visitors across other websites.

4. Cookies and analytics

The public pages of this website do not currently set analytics or advertising cookies. The site does not include third-party advertising or social media tracking scripts.

The content management area used by authorised administrators may use authentication cookies or browser storage provided by Netlify Identity and the content management system. Those tools apply to administrators and are not part of the public browsing experience.

5. How information is used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, provide product information, maintain business records, protect the website, and comply with applicable obligations.

SAVORLEN does not sell personal information. Information is not used for automated decision-making or targeted advertising through this website.

6. Third-party services

Contact routes and website infrastructure involve third-party services. These currently include:

  • Email services used to receive and respond to messages sent to the published SAVORLEN address.
  • WhatsApp, operated by Meta, when you choose the WhatsApp contact link.
  • GitHub and Netlify for website hosting, source control, and content management authentication.

When you use these services, their own terms and privacy policies apply. SAVORLEN does not control how those providers process information independently of the SAVORLEN account.

7. Data retention

Enquiries and related correspondence are kept only for as long as needed to respond, maintain necessary business records, and meet applicable obligations. Server logs are retained according to the hosting provider’s settings and practices.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information connected to the website and its contact channels. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Your choices

You may contact SAVORLEN to ask about the information you have sent, request a correction, or request deletion, subject to applicable record-keeping and legal requirements. You can also choose not to use a particular contact route.

To make a request, use the contact details at the end of this policy and describe the information concerned.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website, contact channels, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top of the page shows the latest revision. Continued use of the website after an update means the revised policy applies.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to main.savorlen@gmail.com. You can also call or message +91 98424 98324.
