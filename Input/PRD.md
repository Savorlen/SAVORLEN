**# 1. Executive Summary**



**SAVORLEN is a consumer-facing packaged food ingredients and spice-blend brand. Its planned product catalogue includes items such as fish masala and other spice blends.**



**The SAVORLEN website will serve as the brand's official digital presence. It must introduce the brand, present its products, provide accurate product information, establish a consistent and credible visual identity, and make it easy for interested customers to contact the business.**



**The website will be built using static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a genuine multi-page architecture. Decap CMS will provide an interface for managing the product catalogue without requiring manual editing of the public website for every routine product update.**



**The website must be designed around four priorities:**



**1. \*\*Brand credibility:\*\* Present SAVORLEN professionally and consistently.**

**2. \*\*Product discovery:\*\* Allow visitors to browse products and understand what is offered.**

**3. \*\*Customer enquiries:\*\* Provide reliable email, phone, and WhatsApp contact routes.**

**4. \*\*Maintainability:\*\* Make product updates manageable through Decap CMS.**



**The website must never compensate for missing business information with fabricated claims, fictional products, fake reviews, invented prices, or unsupported promises.**

**# 8. Product Catalogue Requirements**



**## 8.1 Content Management**



**Products must be managed through Decap CMS.**



**The product catalogue is the source of truth for public product entries. The implementation must define how CMS content is converted into, or consumed by, the static website.**



**The public website must not require manually editing HTML every time a product changes.**



**## 8.2 Product Data Model**



**| Field | Requirement |**

**|---|---|**

**| Product Name | Mandatory |**

**| Display Image (Thumbnail) | Image field |**

**| Description | Product description |**

**| Price | Optional until the pricing rules are confirmed |**

**| Slashed Price | Optional |**

**| Inventory | In Stock, Out of Stock, or numeric quantity |**

**| Category | Product category |**



**The user has not explicitly confirmed whether the display image, description, and category must all be mandatory. Establish suitable validation rules and ask for approval where necessary.**



**## 8.3 Product Name**



**- Required.**

**- Must not be empty.**

**- Must be displayed consistently.**

**- Must not be silently replaced by an invented name.**

**- Must be safe for use in page titles, links, and structured data.**



**## 8.4 Display Image**



**- Use the configured CMS image field.**

**- Store images in a consistent, documented location.**

**- Optimise image sizes for performance.**

**- Preserve aspect ratio.**

**- Use meaningful alternative text where appropriate.**

**- Handle missing images without broken image icons.**



**A default fallback visual may be used, but it must not pretend to be an actual photograph of the product.**



**## 8.5 Description**



**Use only supplied and approved product information.**



**Descriptions must not invent:**

**- Ingredients.**

**- Taste claims.**

**- Health benefits.**

**- Manufacturing methods.**

**- Certifications.**

**- Product origin.**

**- Cooking results.**

**- Nutritional information.**



**## 8.6 Price and Slashed Price**



**Pricing rules require confirmation before final launch.**



**The implementation must:**

**- Avoid inventing a price.**

**- Avoid presenting missing prices as zero.**

**- Avoid displaying a discount without a valid current and reference price.**

**- Validate numeric values.**

**- Use a confirmed currency format.**

**- Avoid implying an offer is time-limited unless its terms are known.**



**## 8.7 Inventory**



**Accepted inventory inputs:**

**- `In Stock`**

**- `Out of Stock`**

**- Numeric quantity**



**Display exact quantities publicly only if approved.**



**If inventory is missing or invalid, do not automatically label the product as in stock.**



**## 8.8 Category**



**Allow products to be assigned to a category through Decap CMS.**



**Category filtering should be generated from actual published category data where practical.**



**Do not create categories for products that do not exist.**



**## 8.9 Product URLs**



**Use stable, readable URLs.**



**If a product name changes, do not unnecessarily break previously published links.**



**Where stable product identifiers or slugs are used, validate uniqueness and define how conflicting slugs are handled.**



**Missing or unpublished products must not return fabricated content.**

**# 10. Technical Architecture**



**## 10.1 Public Website**



**The public website must use:**

**- HTML5.**

**- CSS3.**

**- Vanilla JavaScript.**

**- Optional Tailwind CSS where appropriate.**

**- Static hosting.**



**A build step may be used to optimise assets or generate static product pages, provided the deployed output remains a static website.**



**Do not require a continuously running application server to render public pages.**



**## 10.2 Multi-Page Requirement**



**Each major page must be independently accessible.**



**The site must not be implemented as a single HTML page with anchor sections pretending to be separate pages.**



**Direct URL access, refreshes, navigation, and browser back/forward behaviour must work as expected.**



**## 10.3 Decap CMS**



**Provide an administrative interface under `/admin/`.**



**The CMS must support:**

**- Product creation.**

**- Product editing.**

**- Product publication.**

**- Image management.**

**- Inventory updates.**

**- Category management through product entries.**

**- Appropriate required-field validation.**



**Authentication and repository publishing must be configured securely for the selected hosting platform.**



**The CMS must never expose private credentials to the public website.**



**## 10.4 Data Pipeline**



**Define the complete flow:**



**1. An authorised editor creates or updates a product.**

**2. Decap CMS saves the change to the configured content source.**

**3. The configured build or content-generation workflow processes the published data.**

**4. The public website reads the updated product information.**

**5. The deployment publishes the updated static assets.**

**6. The result is checked on the live site.**



**If the selected hosting and CMS configuration requires a build trigger, implement it rather than assuming updates happen automatically.**



**## 10.5 Failure Handling**



**The website must handle:**

**- Missing product data.**

**- Missing images.**

**- Invalid inventory.**

**- Invalid pricing.**

**- Unpublished products.**

**- Missing product identifiers.**

**- Failed or incomplete deployments.**



**Errors must not silently turn into fake data.**

**# 12. SEO Requirements**



**## 12.1 Page Metadata**



**Each public page must have:**

**- A unique title.**

**- A useful meta description.**

**- A canonical URL using the confirmed production domain.**

**- An appropriate heading structure.**

**- Relevant Open Graph metadata.**



**Do not copy the same title and description across every page.**



**## 12.2 Sitemap**



**Generate and validate `sitemap.xml`.**



**It must:**

**- Use the confirmed production domain.**

**- Include appropriate canonical public URLs.**

**- Exclude administrative pages and non-indexable content.**

**- Exclude draft and unpublished products.**

**- Avoid broken or redirected URLs where practical.**

**- Be updated when public pages and products change.**



**## 12.3 Robots.txt**



**Create and validate `robots.txt`.**



**It must:**

**- Allow crawling of intended public pages and assets.**

**- Reference the sitemap using its full production URL.**

**- Avoid accidentally blocking important CSS, JavaScript, or images.**

**- Avoid exposing private content as if robots.txt were an access-control mechanism.**



**CMS access must be protected through actual authentication, not robots.txt.**



**## 12.4 Structured Data**



**Use structured data only when the required facts are available and accurate.**



**Potential types include:**

**- Organization.**

**- WebSite.**

**- BreadcrumbList.**

**- Product, where applicable.**



**Do not invent ratings, reviews, offers, stock status, business addresses, prices, or identifiers for structured data.**



**Ensure that structured data matches visible page content.**



**Do not promise rich search results or guaranteed rankings.**



**## 12.5 Search Indexing**



**- Ensure intended public pages are indexable.**

**- Avoid accidental `noindex` directives.**

**- Avoid duplicate canonical URLs.**

**- Avoid indexing draft content.**

**- Validate metadata and sitemap output before launch.**



**The production domain must be confirmed before final canonical URLs are published.**
